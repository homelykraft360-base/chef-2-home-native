## Description (≤4,000 characters)

**Chef 2 Home** brings predictable, curated weekly meal planning into one calm mobile experience—whether you’re getting dinner rhythms sorted at home or managing how often your chef visits.

**What you can do**

- **Sign in quickly** with your phone number or Google so you can pick up where you left off.
- **Browse Meals** and understand how weekly meal selection fits into your Chef 2 Home subscription.
- **Manage your subscription** so visiting cadence and subscription status stay clear.
- **Update account details** with verification steps designed to help protect your profile.

**Designed for clarity**

- Straightforward navigation across Home, Meals, Subscription, and Settings.
- Friendly empty states that explain what unlocks when your subscription is active.
- Account updates paired with verification for safer profile changes.

**Notes**

- Chef 2 Home serves customers with location and logistics constraints—availability may vary by area (e.g. Lagos).
- Features shown require an eligible subscription where applicable.

Questions or support? Reach us through the support URL you publish in App Store Connect.
